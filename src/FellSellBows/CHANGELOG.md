# Fell & Sell Bows: changelog

## 1.0.1

- Fixed: the Heavy Bow now goes through an enemy's block. In 1.0.0 a blocked arrow did no damage.
- Added: works with Fell & Sell Coop. A guest's arrows hit the host's enemies with the same damage, stun and shield piercing, and the partner sees the bow being drawn.
